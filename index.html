06 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How late are you open?
Tuesday to Thursday until 9:00pm, Friday until 8:00pm and Saturday until 6:00pm — so you do not have to leave work early. Closed Sunday and Monday.
How do I book?
Booksy has the live calendar and instant slots, or send a request from this page.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes — kids 1 to 11 are $20 for 25 minutes, and teens 12 to 19 are $30.
Do you do colour?
Yes. Women's colour and haircut is $100 for an hour, and perm or texturize is $65.
What is the cheapest thing you offer?
A line up or beard shaping is $10 and takes about ten minutes.
